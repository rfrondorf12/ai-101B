# Start Here: Vault Sync Workflow

## Goal

Back up my Notion files on a schedule, so I never lose my notes and can access them for projects.

## How to Use This Folder

1. Read `notion-process-map.canvas` to see the steps in order.
2. Follow the step files in order: `01-trigger.md` through `05-human-check.md`.
3. Do one step at a time. After each step, stop and tell me what you did before moving on.

## Rules

- The notion workspace must stay private. Never make it public.
- Only sync files from Notion to my Obsidian vault "AI 101". Do not touch anything else on my computer.
- maintain Notion files' current organization in AI 101 Obsidian vault as it is after importing notes directly from Notion. Match document and database titles across Obsidian and Notion for updates. 
- If something is unclear, ask me. Do not guess.
- Explain what you did in plain language. I am not a programmer.
- Embed images in respective pages, do not import them into AI 101 Obsidian vault as loose .png or .jpg files. If you have to import images from Notion as loose .png or .jpg files, place those files in the folder imported_images.
## What Stays My Decision

- How often the sync runs.
- Which repository it goes to.
- Whether the result is correct.
