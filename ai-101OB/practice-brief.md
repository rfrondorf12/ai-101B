# Practice Brief: Turn a Document into a Study Guide

Use this brief only if you do not have your own material to work with.

## The Situation

Your instructor sent you a document to review before next class. You want a study guide that helps you learn it, not one that does the learning for you.

## Your Material

The Project 2 brief. It is real, it matters to you, and it contains nothing private.

## The Problem

"I receive documents to review from my instructor. How can I organize them and create a study guide without compromising my own thinking?"

## Think About

- What do you do first when you get a document like this? Skim, highlight, take notes?
- Which part of making a study guide should the model help with, such as organizing or formatting?
- Which part must stay yours, such as deciding what matters or explaining ideas in your own words?
- How would you know the study guide actually helped you learn?

## Your Workflow Will Have

- Three to five steps, each with its own step file.
- At least one human check where you decide what stays, what changes, and what gets cut.
- A study guide as the output.
