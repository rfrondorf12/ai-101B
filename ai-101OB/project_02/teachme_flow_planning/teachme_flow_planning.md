# Planning Guide
Work through the workflow you ran last week, in order:

1. **Write your problem as one question.** That is your intent.
2. **List the tools you used, in order.** What did each one do?
3. **Mark where you checked the work.** What did you look at before moving on?
4. **Name what comes out at the end.** The artifact.
5. **Find one loop.** Where did an output become a new input?
6. **Find one leverage point.** Where would a small change matter most downstream?
